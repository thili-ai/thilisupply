# thilisupply

The practice files and templates for **Plan the Festival Stock** — a short course on using an AI assistant to
forecast demand, set inventory, compare carriers and classify a product range for a food company, and on
checking what it gives you.

There is no code here. It is a set of spreadsheets to practise on and two blank templates.

> ⚠️ **A practice company, not a real business.** *Nila Foods* is invented: a fictional Kerala packaged-foods
> company, fictional products, fictional suppliers and carriers. The data is deliberately imperfect, the way
> real exports are. **Do not clean it before you use it.** There are no answers in this folder.

## What is here

| File | Used in | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `sales_6skus_to_aug2025.csv` | Lesson 1 | Weekly units sold for six products, January 2024 to the week of 11 August 2025 |
| `events_calendar.csv` | Lesson 1 | Festival dates, and the Onam Sadya Offer promotion |
| `sku_master.csv` | Lessons 1, 2, 4 | One row per product: price, cost, shelf life, quoted lead time, ordering and holding cost, a note |
| `held_back_actuals.csv` | Lesson 1 | The eight weeks the forecast is scored against. **Do not open it until the lesson tells you to** |
| `weekly_sales_all.csv` | Lessons 2, 4 | Weekly units sold for all 40 products, to the week of 6 October 2025 |
| `receipts.csv` | Lesson 2 | Purchase orders with order and receipt dates |
| `shipments.csv` | Lesson 3 | 1,340 shipments by three carriers on six lanes |
| `memo-template.md` | Lesson 5 | A one-page layout for the memo |
| `claim-ledger.md` | Lesson 5 | A blank table for every number in your memo and where it came from |
| `prompts.md` | All | Where the prompts are collected |

## Where this comes from

**Plan the Festival Stock** is the supply chain lab of *AI for Functions*, a programme for management students
and working managers, on [Learn Fast](https://ask.thili.ai).
